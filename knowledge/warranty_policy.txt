AUTOMOTIVE WARRANTY POLICY

Standard Warranty:
All eligible vehicles receive the standard manufacturer warranty
as defined by the applicable vehicle policy.

Warranty claims must be submitted through an authorized dealer.

Warranty coverage is subject to:
- Vehicle eligibility
- Valid documentation
- Applicable warranty terms
- Exclusion conditions

The sales agent must not promise warranty coverage beyond
the information contained in the approved policy documents.

If a customer asks about a warranty condition that is not
available in the knowledge base, escalate the question to
an authorized dealer representative.
